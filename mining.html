Mechanics only, from the verified v6 source. This page promises no returns and expresses no opinion on future price.

What mining is here

You run a small hash grinder locally, and when a solve lands, a transaction mints tokens against your own ETH deposit at the machine's on-chain NAV, with the lock fee waived. Nothing is minted from nothing: your deposit enters the pool in full and backs the tokens you receive.

Puzzlekeccak256(salt, nonce, your address, epoch) < target — the target is fixed at 2-24 (about 1 in 16.7 million hashes per solve. Measured grinder speed (the site's own Node tool, single core): ~97,000 hashes/s → about 2.9 core-minutes per solve. Run one grinder per core in parallel shells to divide that time, or use a native-keccak grinder (~2M H/s) at ~8–15 s per solve).
Your solve is yoursYour address is inside the puzzle domain: no one can steal, share, or reuse a solve. Epochs rotate by one per solved mint, so a stale solve after anyone's mint is void — grind again.
Entry pricingRetail mint = 100.50% of NAV (0.25% locked + 0.25% creator). Mined mint = 100.25% (lock fee waived to you; the 0.25% creator fee still applies).
BackingYour full deposit enters the pool with your mint. The token's floor is its ETH backing; NAV is computed on-chain every block.
ExitRedeem at NAV at any moment (tiered exit fee, published in the contract, half of it permanently locked to the floor). A humane exit-queue law covers deep-redemption states instead of hard denials.
StakingTokens can also be staked into the book's vault, which splits any donated income 40% stakers / 40% ratchet / 20% defense — split fixed in-contract, adjustable by no one.

Honest notes

Mining here is an entry-discount lane, not passive income: the discount scales with how much ETH you attach to a solve, the puzzle cost does not. The machine never pays anyone out of other participants' money — the conservation law is fuzz-tested. Difficulty is FIXED at deploy (2-24); it is not adjusted. This page describes mechanics; it promises no returns and no APY.
