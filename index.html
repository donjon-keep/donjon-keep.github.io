The machine, in plain terms

DONJON is a token on Base whose value is its ETH backing, not a promise. You mint by depositing ETH; you redeem your tokens back at the machine's NAV at any moment, by sending one transaction. There is no issuer, no admin key, no upgrade path, and no pause switch — the contract is immutable from genesis.

The floorBacked 1:1 by the ETH pool. A sealed genesis lock can never be redeemed — the floor's permanent seed.
The ratchetEvery mint/redeem action pays a fee, and every fee permanently raises NAV. Pumps and dumps both pay the floor.
StakingStaked tokens earn a share of income donated to the book's vault — a fixed, published split (40% stakers / 40% ratchet / 20% defense), set in the contract, adjustable by no one.

What it is not

It is not an interest-bearing account, not an investment vehicle, and not a currency peg. The NAV drifts only when the machine earns fees or receives donations. There is no rate promise — only structure, formulas, and receipts.
