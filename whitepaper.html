DONJON — Machine Specification

Version: 2026-10-13 · Chain: Base (8453) · Contract: 0x67F93E8b7141D32C316C4DC7456f9f0c214702C6 (source verified on Basescan)

1. Abstract

DONJON is an immutable, ETH-backed redeemable token. There is no issuer, no admin key, no upgrade path, and no pause switch. Tokens are minted against ETH deposits and redeemed for ETH by the machine's own formulas, priced at its on-chain NAV. Fees on both directions permanently raise the NAV. This document specifies the mechanics only; it promises no returns and expresses no opinion on future price.

2. Core formulas

Mint (deposit ETH)The machine accepts ETH, and mints token amount = ETH net of fee ÷ NAV. The NAV at that block defines the exchange rate; the minting is formula-priced, so there is no slippage curve and nothing to sandwich on the rails themselves.
Redeem (burn tokens)Sending tokens to the machine redeems ETH = token amount × NAV, net of fee, subject to the free-pool limit: redemptions revert past the locked backing. The locked tranche is never payable.
NAVnav = floorPool ÷ (totalSupply − reservedGuard). The reserved guard is a fixed denominator placeholder carrying no balance; it prevents a post-full-redemption repricing state.
RatchetEvery mint/redeem fee is split: half is locked into the backing pool forever (a permanent NAV ratchet); half books to the creator's fee ledger. Ratchets are monotone between claims.

3. Genesis lock

At deployment the machine was seeded with an initial backing. That seed's tokens are locked at first block inside the immutable floor: they cannot transfer, stake, or redeem. A founder can therefore never dump or redeem the genesis backing — this is a structural property of the code, not a policy.

4. Staking vault

Anyone may donate to the machine's vault. A donation splits by a fixed, in-contract ratio (published on the token site: stakers / ratchet / defense). Tokens staked in the vault earn a proportional share of the staker tranche at claim time. The vault is fully programmatic: no curator, allocator, or rate-setter exists. No APY is promised anywhere; the split is the only rule.

6. Market surface

The machine itself is the primary venue (formula-priced mint/redeem). A thin DONJ/WETH Aerodrome pool exists for price discovery; DEX price below NAV is arbitraged back by mint-and-burn (a standing structural bid). Pool 0x90C0efC657cFd72D9cf6a9aa4Cbef458b0665e17; pair analytics on GeckoTerminal.

7. Risks, stated plainly

Backing is ETH: the USD-value of NAV floats with ETH; the machine's own accounting is ETH-denominated. A full redemption run pays out pro-rata to the free pool and rations/ reverts past the lock — late redeemers cannot break the floor, but they share it. Base sequencer halts pause mint/redeem until the chain resumes. The contract cannot be patched by anyone, including the deployer.
